# Unified Agentic AI, context and CloudOps chapters

September 17, 2026. Authorized refinement of the existing chapters; local-only.

## Problem and result

The agent figure spanned the heading, explanation and evidence rows. Left-side cards determined its height and created excessive vertical space. A long sticky span also separated the diagram from a complete reading of its principles. Context introduced a mint accent and unboxed explanations, while the CloudOps note lacked a comparable visual.

All three now share one composition: heading and supporting narrative on the left, a contained particle diagram on the right, and three defined panels below. Agentic AI uses a 650px maximum figure confined to its overview. Intent / Plan / Evidence remain visible together in a desktop row. No sticky viewport or phase-driven hiding. Context uses the same violet palette and card treatment. Mobile/zoom stack panels and put visual annotations into document flow.

CloudOps has its own matching particle evidence engine: Retrieve → Authorize → Ground → Review, a rotating layered core, four nodes and connected routes. Its point destinations are procedural, independent of likeness; the same canvas/portrait source is reused. Source documents and engine gather before their headings leave view. Native scroll remains reversible; phase controls select/focus an explanation without concealing the others. GSAP reveals prose and panels with restrained depth/movement; pause and reduced/static modes remain available.
